## 📞 How to Find an Attorney (Simple Version)

### Step 1: Google Search (5 minutes)
```
"fintech attorney [your city]"
```
OR
```
"money transmitter lawyer [your state]"
```

### Step 2: Pick 2-3 Options (10 minutes)
- Look for attorneys with "fintech" or "payment" experience
- Check their website - do they mention money transmission?
- Don't overthink this - just pick 2-3 that look relevant

### Step 3: Call or Email (15 minutes)
**What to say:**
> "Hi, I'm building a payment platform and need to understand my legal requirements. Do you offer consultations? What's the cost?"

**That's it.** You don't need to explain everything. Just get them on the phone or email.
